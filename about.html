About

Who We Are

EHA, which stands for Environment, Health, and Advanced Technology Institute, is an independent research organization committed to advancing science and technology in the fields of environmental and public health.

The institute addresses pressing environmental and health challenges through a multidisciplinary approach and fosters innovation through evidence-based research. EHA Institute integrates scientific research, technological development, and community empowerment to develop sustainable, impactful solutions.

EHA Institute is a non-profit organization focused on research, study, and development in the fields of science, technology, and sustainable environmental practices.

Vision

The institute was founded in response to the growing need for sustainable living amid major changes in the environment and in the way people live, with the aim of producing innovative solutions for a healthier, more sustainable future.

Mission & Values

Our core values spell S-U-S-T-A-I-N.

  • S

    Science

    Commitment to evidence-based research and scientific integrity.

  • U

    Unity

    Building interdisciplinary and cross-sector partnerships to co-create solutions.

  • S

    Sustainable

    Prioritizing long-term outcomes for the environment and public health.

  • T

    Technology

    Embracing and developing advanced technologies for complex challenges.

  • A

    Accessibility

    Keeping knowledge, tools and programs inclusive and open.

  • I

    Integrity

    Honesty and transparency in research, partnerships and reporting.

  • N

    Nurture

    Supporting the growth of knowledge, people and communities.

Why Choose EHA?

Behind every initiative at EHA Institute is a multidisciplinary team of researchers, academics, technology practitioners, environmental advocates, and community facilitators united by a shared passion for driving sustainable change.

We believe that cross-disciplinary collaboration is essential to crafting innovative solutions for today’s global challenges. With diverse backgrounds in environmental science, renewable energy, digital technology, and community empowerment, our team works in synergy to design impactful, data-driven, and future-oriented programs.
